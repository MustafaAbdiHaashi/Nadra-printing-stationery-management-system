# Nadra Printing & Stationery Management System

A modern business management application developed for **Nadra Printing & Stationery** to streamline daily business operations, improve inventory control, and provide better visibility into sales and financial performance.

## Overview

The Nadra Management System aims to bring essential business activities into one centralized platform, helping manage stationery products, printing services, customer transactions, and operational records efficiently.

## Key Features

- **Dashboard & Analytics** — Monitor business performance, sales, expenses, and profit metrics.
- **Point of Sale (POS)** — Manage sales transactions efficiently.
- **Sales Management** — Record and track sales and transaction history.
- **Inventory Management** — Organize products, categories, and stock.
- **Customer Management** — Maintain customer records.
- **Printing Order Management** — Organize printing and photocopy orders.
- **Payments & Expenses** — Track payments and business expenses.
- **Responsive Interface** — A modern interface designed for different screen sizes.
- **Light & Dark Themes** — Theme support where implemented.
